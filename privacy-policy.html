Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quotes App (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1) Summary

  • The App may show personalized ads through third-party advertising partners (e.g., Google AdMob).
  • The App may offer subscriptions via Google Play Billing (for Android users).
  • The App may include an optional “Buy me coffee / Support developer” feature that opens an external Stripe payment link.

2) Information we collect

Depending on features you use, we and our service providers may collect:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) used for ad delivery, personalization, frequency capping, and measurement.
  • App activity (e.g., screens viewed, taps/clicks) for analytics, ad performance measurement, and troubleshooting.
  • Diagnostics (e.g., crash logs, performance data).
  • Purchase / subscription information (e.g., subscription status, purchase tokens/receipts) to enable premium access where applicable.
  • Approximate location may be inferred by ad partners from network signals (such as IP address) for ad relevance and fraud prevention.

3) Payments, subscriptions, and tips

Subscriptions and in-app purchases: If you subscribe or buy any in-app digital feature on Android, payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We may receive confirmation information (such as purchase tokens and subscription status) to unlock premium features.

“Buy me coffee / Support developer”: If you choose to support the developer, the App opens an external Stripe-hosted payment page (Payment Link) in your browser. Any payment details you provide are processed by Stripe. The App does not store your card details. (Note: this support payment does not unlock in-app digital features unless provided via Google Play Billing.)

4) How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the App features.
  • To serve and measure ads (including personalized ads where enabled and permitted).
  • To process subscriptions/purchases and verify premium entitlement.
  • To improve reliability, fix bugs, and prevent fraud/abuse.

5) Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers to operate the App, including:

  • Advertising partners (e.g., Google AdMob) to deliver and measure ads.
  • Analytics / crash reporting providers to understand performance and fix issues.
  • Payment processors (Google Play for subscriptions; Stripe for external support payments).

We do not sell your personal information.

6) Your choices

  • You can limit ad personalization from your Android device settings (Ads/Privacy settings vary by device).
  • You can cancel subscriptions through Google Play subscription settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time.

7) Data retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above and as required by law or platform requirements. Service providers may retain data according to their own policies.

8) Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
